For anyone who looks after shared money: class funds, neighbourhood dues, an
arisan, a team's kas. Create a *cashplan* with a link of your choosing, record
income and expenses, and share the link.

- **Append-only history.** Entries can't be edited or deleted, which the
  database enforces, so everyone sees the same numbers.
- **Who has paid.** Set a recurring due and a member list, and Cashflow shows
  who is paid up, partly paid, or hasn't paid yet.
- **Per-payer breakdown**, totals, balance, and search through the history.
- **Anyone with the link can view.** No account needed to check the books.

The interface is in Indonesian.
