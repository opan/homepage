A club's season ranking usually lives in a spreadsheet that one person keeps up
to date. Tally replaces it, starting with badminton but working for any sport or
any group that keeps score.

- **Two ways to score.** Enter each player's points per session, the same way the
  spreadsheet did, or shuffle players into pairs and record match scores.
- **Bring your spreadsheet.** Import an existing season from the club's points sheet.
- **Share the ranking.** Send a link, or a poster image straight to the group chat.
- **Indonesian and English** interface.
